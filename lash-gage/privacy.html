Privacy Policy for LashGage

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

LashGage ("the Application") is an offline-first mechanical engineering and automotive utility developed by KimRosil App Studio. We are committed to complete privacy and transparency.

1. Zero Data Collection

LashGage operates 100% locally on your Android device. The Application:

  • Does NOT collect, transmit, or harvest personal information.
  • Does NOT use third-party analytics, crash reporting beacons, or tracking SDKs.
  • Does NOT require account registration or login credentials.
  • Does NOT request network or internet permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`).

2. Local Storage

Any setup logs, backlash records, or user preferences entered in the Application are stored exclusively in the private application sandbox via SQLite. This data never leaves your device.

3. Permissions

The Application requests only standard runtime features necessary for tactile shop feedback:

  • `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Provides subtle haptic feedback upon dial indicator bounds and button presses.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

  • Email: eliakimrosil@gmail.com
