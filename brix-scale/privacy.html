Privacy Policy for BrixScale

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Developer: KimRosil App Studio Company (Eliakim Rosil)

1. 100% Offline Architecture

BrixScale operates completely offline on your Android device. It does not request `android.permission.INTERNET` and cannot transmit any data over local networks or the internet.

2. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, store, share, or monetize any personal information, telemetry, analytics, crash logs, or device identifiers.

3. Local Device Storage

All brew recipes, refractometer measurements, and appearance preferences you enter are stored strictly inside your device's private local SQLite database and SharedPreferences storage. Clearing app data or uninstalling the application permanently removes all local records.

4. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, visit [https://apps.kimrosil.com/](https://apps.kimrosil.com/).
