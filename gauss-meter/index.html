Overview & Core Capabilities

GaussMeter is an offline 3-axis magnetometer, Microtesla (µT) and Milligauss (mG) flux meter, and ferrous metal / wall stud proximity locator built in pure native Kotlin and powered by your phone's hardware Hall-effect geomagnetic sensor (Sensor.TYPE_MAGNETIC_FIELD).

Designed for electricians, remodelers, drywall installers, audio technicians, and electronics engineers, GaussMeter isolates net ferrous magnetic anomalies above the local Earth geomagnetic field without ads, subscriptions, or network permissions.

KEY FEATURES:
• Live 3-Axis Hardware Magnetometer: Real-time Bx, By, Bz vector components and net 3D magnitude in Microtesla (µT) and Milligauss (mG).
• Earth Baseline Tare Zeroing: One-tap Earth Tare subtracts local geomagnetic background (25–65 µT) to isolate net ferrous perturbations (+∆ µT).
• Ferrous Stud & Conduit Proximity Bar: Visual 180-degree flux gauge paired with a color-coded proximity bar and inverse-cube dipole depth estimator (~cm).
• Vector Coupling Modes: Switch between 3D Composite Vector, Z-Depth Axis (perpendicular to wall surface), and XY In-Plane sensing.
• Field & Hardware Benchmarks: Instant presets for Earth Background (48 µT), Drywall Screw & Steel Stud (115 µT), Steel Conduit & Transformer (190 µT), and Neodymium Speaker Magnet (420 µT).
• Offline SQLite Scan Log: Save, inspect, and reload multi-wall and equipment magnetic scans completely offline.
• Magnetite Obsidian & Laboratory Quartz Themes: Switch seamlessly between Dark and Light modes.
