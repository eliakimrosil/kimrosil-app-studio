Privacy Policy for PPL Workout

Last updated: October 21, 2026

KimRosil App Studio built PPL Workout as a 100% offline, free utility. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection & Storage

PPL Workout does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, telemetry, location data, or device identifiers.

All workout logs, exercise records, weights, reps, and settings are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database. No data ever leaves your device.

2. Permissions

PPL Workout uses standard Android system features solely for on-device workout functionality:

  • `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Triggers tactile haptic feedback when rest intervals finish.
  • `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Displays rest timer progress while using other apps.

No network permissions or advertising IDs are requested or used.

3. Third-Party Services & Zero Tracking

The application contains zero third-party software development kits (SDKs), zero analytics platforms, and zero advertising networks.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this policy, contact KimRosil App Studio at [https://apps.kimrosil.com/](https://apps.kimrosil.com/).
