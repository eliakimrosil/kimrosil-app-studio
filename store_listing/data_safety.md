## 1. Google Play Console Data Safety Overview
Use these exact responses when completing the **Google Play Console > App content > Data safety** section:

| Questionnaire Prompt | Required Answer | Explanation / Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** | **No** | 100% offline utility. Zero network requests, zero SDK telemetry, zero data collected or shared. |
| **Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** | **N/A** | No data is collected or transmitted across any network. |
| **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?** | **N/A** | No personal user data is ever collected or stored remotely. |

---

## 2. Zero-Collection Guarantee
The Application explicitly **DOES NOT** collect, store, or share any user data:
- ❌ **Location:** No location access requested or collected.
- ❌ **Personal info:** No names, emails, phone numbers, or account IDs.
- ❌ **Financial info:** No payment data, credit info, or transactions.
- ❌ **Health & Fitness:** No health or medical records.
- ❌ **Messages & Photos:** No media, files, or messages accessed.
- ❌ **App activity & Performance:** Zero third-party analytics or crash trackers.
- ❌ **Device or other IDs:** Zero device identifiers, advertising IDs, or SIM data collected.

---

## 3. Sandboxed On-Device Operation
All calculations, records, and preferences remain strictly within the sandboxed local application storage on the user's device and are never transmitted over any network.

---

## 4. Google Play Console Form Submission Steps
1. Navigate to **Google Play Console** → Select `CombGauge Wet Film Calculator` (`com.kimrosil.combgauge`).
2. Go to **Policy and programs** → **App content** → **Data safety** → Click **Manage** (or **Start**).
3. Under **Data collection and security**:
   - Select **No** for "Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?".
4. Click **Next** → Review the summary (all categories show 'No data collected').
5. Click **Save** / **Submit**.
