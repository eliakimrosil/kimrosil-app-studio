## 2. Zero-Collection Guarantee
The Application explicitly **DOES NOT** collect, store, or share any user data:
- ❌ **Location:** No location access requested or collected.
- ❌ **Personal info:** No names, emails, phone numbers, or account IDs.
- ❌ **Financial info:** No payment data, credit info, or transactions.
- ❌ **Health & Fitness:** No health or medical records.
- ❌ **Messages & Photos:** No media, files, or messages accessed.
- ❌ **App activity & Performance:** Zero third-party analytics or crash trackers.
- ❌ **Device or other IDs:** Zero device identifiers, advertising IDs, or SIM data collected.

---

## 3. Sandboxed On-Device Operation
All calculations, lookup tables, and preferences remain strictly within the sandboxed local application storage on the user's device and are never transmitted over any network.
