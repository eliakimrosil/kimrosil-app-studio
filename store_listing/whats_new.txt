Initial release of TaperGauge:
• Machine lathe taper angle (compound rest swivel) and TPF/TPI solver.
• Tailstock set-over offset calculation for turning between centers.
• Morse Taper (MT0-MT7), Brown & Sharpe, Jacobs, and R8 presets.
• 2D dynamic workpiece taper profile visualizer.
• Full offline operation with Day/Night/Auto themes and Quick Settings tile.
