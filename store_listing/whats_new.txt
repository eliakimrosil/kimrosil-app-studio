Initial release of DeciGauge v1.0.0:
• Real-time sound pressure level (SPL) meter with 44.1 kHz PCM audio sampling.
• IEC 61672-1 A-weighting, C-weighting, and Z-flat frequency filters.
• Fast (125ms), Slow (1s), and Impulse response ballistics.
• NIOSH and OSHA daily noise dose tracking with remaining safe exposure time.
• Laboratory HUD canvas with analog needle gauge, peak hold, and 8-band audio spectrum visualizer.
• Offline SQLite session logging and Quick Settings tile integration.
• Dark, Light, and Automatic theme support.
