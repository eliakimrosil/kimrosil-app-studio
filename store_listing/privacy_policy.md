# Privacy Policy for ConduitFill

**Effective Date:** October 2026
**Developer:** KimRosil App Studio Company

## Overview
ConduitFill is an offline-first electrical engineering calculator developed by KimRosil App Studio Company. The application operates entirely locally on your device without transmitting data over any network.

## Permissions & Data Collection
• Zero internet or network permissions: ConduitFill makes no network connections.
• Zero telemetry or personal data collection: No names, analytics, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers are collected.
• Local Storage: Conduit job runs and calculations are stored solely within a local SQLite database on your device and are never synchronized off-device.
