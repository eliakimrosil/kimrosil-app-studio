# Privacy Policy for PlumbBob

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026  
**KimRosil App Studio Company** (Developer: Eliakim Rosil)

PlumbBob is engineered as a 100% offline-first utility application. We firmly believe your jobsite calculations, measurements, and device data belong exclusively to you.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
PlumbBob does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information, location telemetry, device identifiers, or usage statistics.

### 2. Zero External Network Requests
The application contains no third-party tracking SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no analytics libraries. PlumbBob performs zero network connections and functions entirely without an active internet connection.

### 3. Local Storage Only
All saved trench run records and calculation presets are stored strictly on your local device within an offline SQLite database. This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you choose to uninstall the application or clear app storage.

### 4. Permissions
• `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Utilized solely to provide tactile haptic feedback when tapping calculation steppers and mode switches.
• `android.permission.BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE`: Utilized to provide the optional Quick Settings tile in your Android notification shade.
