# Privacy Policy for BendSpec

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

BendSpec is built and maintained by KimRosil App Studio Company as an offline-first freeware utility.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
BendSpec does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, or analytics.

## 2. 100% Offline Architecture
All calculations, material parameters, flange layouts, and saved ledger records are executed and stored entirely locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences and SQLite. No internet connection is requested or required.

## 3. Permissions
- No runtime permissions required.
