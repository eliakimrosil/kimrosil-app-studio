# Privacy Policy for OhmBand

**Effective Date:** October 2026
**Developer:** KimRosil App Studio Company

## Overview
OhmBand is an offline-first electronic engineering calculator developed by KimRosil App Studio Company. The application operates entirely locally on your device without transmitting data over any network.

## Permissions & Data Collection
• Zero internet or network permissions: OhmBand makes no network connections.
• Zero personal data collection: No names, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers are collected.
• Local Storage: Resistor component logs are stored solely within a local SQLite database on your device and are never synchronized off-device.
