# Privacy Policy for CueScroll

**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Developer:** KimRosil App Studio Company (Eliakim Rosil)

## 1. Overview
CueScroll is an offline teleprompter. It does not collect, transmit, store off-device, or share any personal information.

## 2. Permissions & Data Access
- **No Internet Access:** CueScroll does not declare the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission and cannot communicate with remote servers.
- **Vibration:** The manifest declares `android.permission.VIBRATE` for optional haptic feedback.
- **Local Storage:** Scripts you write are stored in a private SQLite database on your device. Settings are stored in private app preferences. Nothing is transmitted externally. Removing the app or using "Erase all data" deletes them.
- **Clipboard:** Clipboard text is read only when you press PASTE in the editor.

## 3. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising
CueScroll contains zero third-party SDKs, zero analytics engines, and zero advertising networks.
