# Privacy Policy for DeciGauge

**Effective Date:** October 2026
**Developer:** KimRosil App Studio Company

## Overview
DeciGauge is committed to complete user privacy. As an offline-first utility, DeciGauge operates locally on your device without transmitting data over any network.

## Microphone Permission
DeciGauge requests access to the device microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) solely to calculate real-time sound pressure levels, frequency weightings, and noise dose metrics in device memory.
• Audio samples are processed strictly in real-time RAM buffers.
• No audio recordings, voice recordings, or sound files are ever saved to disk or transmitted off the device.
• Microphone stream terminates immediately when the app is paused or closed.

## Data Storage & Permissions
• All measurement history and session records are stored solely in a local SQLite database on your device.
• The app requires no internet access, accounts, cloud sync, or third-party analytics SDKs.
• You have full control to clear all session logs at any time.
