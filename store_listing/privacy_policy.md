# Privacy Policy for WeldBead

**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

WeldBead is developed by KimRosil App Studio as an offline-first trade utility.

### Data Collection and Usage
• **Zero Data Collection**: WeldBead does not collect, transmit, store on remote servers, or share any personal information, location data, or telemetry.
• **Local Storage Only**: All weld calculation parameters, presets, and pass history are stored strictly on the local device using SQLite and Android SharedPreferences. No data ever leaves your device.
• **No Network Permissions**: WeldBead requires zero internet permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET` is not requested). It operates completely offline.
• **No Advertisements or Analytics**: WeldBead contains no advertisements, third-party analytics SDKs, tracking libraries, or background monitoring services.
