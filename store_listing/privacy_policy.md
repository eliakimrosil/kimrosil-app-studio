# Privacy Policy for KR Live Studio

*Last Updated: October 25, 2026*

This privacy policy applies to the Android mobile application **KR Live Studio** (package: `com.kimrosil.krlivestudio`), developed and published by Eliakim Rosil ("we", "us", or "our") under KimRosil App Studio (https://apps.kimrosil.com).

The Application is distributed via the Google Play Store, official releases, and https://apps.kimrosil.com/kim-live-studio/.

---

### 1. Core Architecture & Direct Transmission
KR Live Studio is a direct broadcasting utility designed to stream display content, floating camera overlay, and microphone audio directly to standard RTMP and RTMPS endpoints chosen by the user.

- **Direct Ingest Only:** Audio and video packets generated by the device hardware encoders (MediaCodec) are transmitted directly across standard network sockets (TCP/TLS) to the user's designated RTMP/RTMPS ingest server. No intermediary developer servers, proxies, or cloud relays exist. We never intercept, inspect, record, or store your broadcast stream.
- **Advertising & Third-Party SDKs:** Broadcast streams are transmitted directly between your device and your designated RTMP ingest server without intermediary cloud harvesting. When third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) are integrated, standard advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID) and non-personally identifiable diagnostic signals may be processed solely to deliver relevant advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
- **No Account Required:** Full application functionality is provided without sign-ups, accounts, logins, or cloud subscriptions.

---

### 2. Device Permissions & Usage

The Application requests only standard Android permissions strictly necessary to perform direct live streaming:

- **`RECORD_AUDIO`**: Captures microphone commentary to mix with broadcast audio. Audio capture operates only while active broadcasting is engaged.
- **`CAMERA`**: Enables the optional floating picture-in-picture (PiP) facecam preview. Camera frames are rendered locally into the camera preview surface and are never accessed when facecam is disabled.
- **`FOREGROUND_SERVICE` & Subtypes (`mediaProjection`, `camera`, `microphone`)**: Android 14+ foreground service types required to maintain uninterrupted stream encoding while navigating into games, presentations, or other applications. An ongoing status notification remains visible in your notification shade during active streaming.
- **`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` ("Display Over Other Apps")**: Allows the floating facecam preview and micro-telemetry badge to float over other running applications.
- **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`**: Displays ongoing status notifications indicating active broadcast uptime, bitrate, and a quick-stop action.
- **`INTERNET`**: Required to establish the outbound RTMP/RTMPS socket connection to your destination broadcast server.
- **`VIBRATE`**: Provides tactile haptic feedback when interacting with studio controls.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Stream Key Security

- **Sandboxed Local Storage:** Your RTMP ingest URLs, resolution/bitrate presets, and private stream keys are saved exclusively within Android's sandboxed `SharedPreferences` on your physical device (`com.kimrosil.krlivestudio`).
- **No Background Clipboard Polling:** KR Live Studio does not passively read your system clipboard. Stream keys are imported only when you explicitly tap the **[PASTE]** button.
- **Complete Data Deletion:** Clearing application data in Android Settings (`Settings > Apps > KR Live Studio > Storage > Clear Data`) or uninstalling the app permanently and irreversibly deletes all stored keys and configuration profiles.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services & Streaming Destinations

When you broadcast to third-party live streaming platforms (such as YouTube Live, Twitch, Kick, Facebook Live, TikTok Live, or custom RTMP ingest servers), your transmission is governed by that platform's respective Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. KR Live Studio does not manage, store, or delete content once transmitted to third-party platforms.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

KR Live Studio is a utility engineered for general audiences and content creators aged 13 and older (or 18+ where applicable). It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
