# Privacy Policy for ClampPitch

**Last updated:** October 28, 2026

ClampPitch is built and maintained by KimRosil App Studio Company as an offline-first freeware utility.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
ClampPitch does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, or analytics.

## 2. 100% Offline Architecture
All calculations, timer states, and user preferences are executed and stored entirely locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences and SQLite. No internet connection is requested or required.

## 3. Permissions
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback when countdown timers expire or adjustment steppers are pressed.
- `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Used exclusively to alert you when an adhesive open-time countdown finishes while the application is in the background.
