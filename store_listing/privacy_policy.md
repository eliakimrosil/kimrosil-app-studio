# Privacy Policy for DipLink

**Effective Date:** October 22, 2026  
**Developer:** KimRosil App Studio Company (Eliakim Rosil)  
**Application:** DipLink (Offline Fuel Tank Dipstick Calibration)

## 1. Zero Data Collection
DipLink operates 100% offline on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, usage telemetry, hardware identifiers, location data, or dipstick measurement entries.

## 2. Network & Permissions
DipLink does not require, request, or use the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission. All mathematical calculations, empirical calibrations, and local preferences run entirely in local memory on your device.
The only permission requested is `android.permission.VIBRATE` to provide subtle tactile feedback when tapping stepper buttons.

## 3. Local Storage
Your selected storage tank model, theme mode preference (System/Light/Dark), and tactile haptics toggle are stored locally in private Android `SharedPreferences`. This data never leaves your device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

## 4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising
DipLink contains zero third-party software development kits (SDKs), zero advertisements, and zero background analytics trackers.

## 5. Contact
For questions regarding this privacy policy, visit [https://apps.kimrosil.com/](https://apps.kimrosil.com/) or contact KimRosil App Studio.
