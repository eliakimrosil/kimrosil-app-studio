# Privacy Policy for HydraFlow

**Last updated:** October 28, 2026

HydraFlow is built and maintained by KimRosil App Studio Company as an offline-first freeware utility.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
HydraFlow does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, or analytics.

## 2. 100% Offline Architecture
All calculations, cylinder dimensions, and user preferences are executed and stored entirely locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences. No internet connection is requested or required.

## 3. Permissions
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback when calculation triggers or steppers are pressed.
