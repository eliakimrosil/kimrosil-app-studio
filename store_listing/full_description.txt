Never guess or forget clothing measurements while shopping again. SizeVault is a private, offline-first personal size manager designed to keep track of shoe sizes, shirt fits, pant measurements, and ring dimensions for yourself, your partner, your children, and extended family members. Whether you are shopping holiday sales, picking up back-to-school outfits, or finding the perfect surprise gift, SizeVault gives you immediate access to accurate measurements without needing an active internet connection. Key Features: • Comprehensive Family Profiles: Create distinct visual profiles for yourself, partner, children, and parents to keep sizes organized. • Detailed Measurement Tracking: Record shoe sizes across US, EU, and cm standards, alongside shirt sizes, waist and inseam measurements, jacket cuts, and ring sizes. • Custom Brand Fit Notes: Document specific fit quirks directly on each profile, such as specific brands running narrow or preferences for relaxed fits. • Convenient Text Sharing: Generate cleanly formatted measurement summaries ready to send to relatives or friends via standard messaging apps. • Offline-First Privacy: Your family data is stored directly on your device. SizeVault operates completely offline without tracking, analytics, or mandatory account creation. SizeVault removes the guesswork from apparel shopping and gift buying, ensuring you always select the right fit the first time.
