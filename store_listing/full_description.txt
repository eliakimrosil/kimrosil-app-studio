ScreenStream RTSP Broadcaster turns your Android device into a high-performance, real-time video streaming server. Broadcast your screen activity directly over your local Wi-Fi network using standard RTSP (Real-Time Streaming Protocol) with minimal delay. Whether you need to demonstrate mobile workflows, monitor device activity, present mobile presentations on a desktop monitor, or feed live gameplay into production software like OBS Studio or VLC, ScreenStream delivers consistent, hardware-accelerated performance. Key Features: - Ultra-Low Latency: Optimized H.264/AVC hardware encoding ensures fluid, near-instant screen mirroring. - Local Network First: Streams operate entirely within your local area network (LAN/WLAN) without sending data to external cloud servers. - Standard Protocol Support: Connect effortlessly from any RTSP-compliant media player, including VLC, MPV, OBS Studio, and smart displays. - Configurable Stream Parameters: Adjust video resolution, frame rate (FPS), and bitrate to balance quality and network bandwidth. - Internal and Microphone Audio: Capture and transmit synchronized audio streams alongside high-definition video. - Privacy Focused: No user tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no unnecessary background telemetry. - Clean Control Interface: Start and stop broadcasts with a single tap, accompanied by clear IP and port connection details. Designed for developers, content creators, network administrators, and educators seeking a dependable, zero-friction screen streaming solution across local environments.
