FlareGage delivers accurate flare dimension reference tables, diameter tolerances, and fitting clearance data directly to technicians working in HVAC, automotive, refrigeration, and hydraulic systems. Fabricating single, double, or bubble flares requires exact measurements. An undersized flare risks blow-out under operating pressures exceeding 400 PSI in modern refrigerants or 1500 PSI in hydraulic brake systems. An oversized flare risks thread galling, uneven seating, and metal fatigue. FlareGage eliminates guesswork with reliable dimensional standards calculated for copper, aluminum, and steel tubing. Designed for real-world mechanical environments, FlareGage operates fully offline with instant access controls, allowing quick visual checks without navigating slow menus or complex charts. Core Capabilities: - Standardized Flare Reference: View accurate outer diameter, head depth, and shoulder angle specifications across common tube sizes. - Multi-Industry Standards: Access specifications for 45-degree SAE flare, 37-degree JIC flare, 45-degree double flare, and ISO/DIN bubble flare configurations. - Tolerance Range Indicators: Review minimum and maximum allowable diameter limits to verify flare nut retention and prevent leaks. - Quick Measurement Access: Designed for rapid checks between cutting, deburring, and flaring operations in the shop or field. - Zero Data Collection: Operates completely offline with no network permissions, accounts, or analytics tracking. Ensure proper seal integrity on every brake line, mini-split lineset, and refrigeration circuit with FlareGage.
