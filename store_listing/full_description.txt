TorqueSpec delivers instant, offline bolt torque specifications and fastener engineering data directly to your device. Built specifically for field service technicians, diesel mechanics, fabricators, and machinists working in low-connectivity environments such as pole barns, pits, and remote machine shops. Eliminate guesswork and damaged threads with immediate access to standard tightening values, thread pitches, tensile ratings, and fastener dimensions without requiring cellular reception or account sign-ins. Core Features: - Comprehensive Torque Tables: Standard tightening values for Metric (Class 8.8, 10.9, 12.9) and Imperial SAE (Grade 2, 5, 8) fasteners across dry and lubricated conditions. - Fast Offline Search: Instant query engine powered by local database indexing allows you to locate thread specs and bolt diameters in milliseconds. - Mechanical Specifications: Detailed data on coarse and fine thread pitches, tap drill sizes, proof load limits, and recommended clamping force. - Zero Connectivity Required: Fully self-contained local database functions seamlessly underground, inside metal facilities, or in remote job sites. - Clean Workshop Interface: High-contrast layout designed for quick reading and single-tap reference while wearing shop gloves. - Complete Privacy: No user tracking, no third-party analytics, and no background network connections. Whether torquing cylinder heads, servicing agricultural machinery, or assembling structural steel, TorqueSpec provides reliable mechanical reference data instantly on the shop floor.
