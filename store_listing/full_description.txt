HydraFlow is a dedicated, 100% offline fluid power calculation suite and cylinder telemetry assistant for heavy equipment mechanics, hydraulic technicians, machine builders, and agricultural engineers.

Calculate hydraulic cylinder push and pull force tonnage, extension and retraction speeds, total cycle times, hydraulic horsepower, and hose line velocities across industrial systems.

KEY CAPABILITIES

1. Cylinder Force & Tonnage Calculator
• Dual Units: US Customary (Inches, PSI, US Tons, GPM) and Metric (Millimeters, Bar, Metric Tonnes / kN, L/min).
• Full cylinder telemetry: Bore diameter, rod diameter, and system operating pressure.
• Extension (Push) Force: Computes full piston face area, push force in Tons, pounds force, and kilonewtons.
• Retraction (Pull) Force: Computes net annular area (bore area minus rod area) and true retract pull force.
• Rod displacement ratio and differential area percentage.

2. Stroke Cycle Speed & Hydraulic Power
• Enter cylinder stroke length and pump flow rate (GPM or L/min).
• Extension Speed: Inches per second (or mm/s) and extension time in seconds.
• Retraction Speed: Faster return speed accounting for reduced annular fluid volume.
• Total cycle duration (extend + retract time).
• Hydraulic Horsepower: Calculates required fluid horsepower (HP / kW) at target operating pressure.

3. SAE J517 Hose Line Velocity Auditor
• Hose inside diameter (1/4" to 2" / -04 to -32 dash sizes) vs pump flow rate.
• Line Type Compliance:
  - Pressure Lines (Recommended 10–15 ft/s or 3–4.5 m/s)
  - Return Lines (Recommended 5–10 ft/s or 1.5–3 m/s)
  - Pump Suction Lines (Recommended 2–4 ft/s or 0.6–1.2 m/s to prevent pump cavitation)
• Instant visual compliance status badge: Optimal, Warning, or High Cavitation Risk.
