TankPulse is an offline-first fuel log, vehicle mileage tracker, and fuel economy calculator designed for speed, clarity, and complete data privacy. Log fill-ups in seconds right at the pump without waiting for network connectivity or slow cloud synchronizations. TankPulse operates entirely on your device, requiring zero account sign-ups, zero logins, and zero unnecessary background data usage. KEY CAPABILITIES • Quick Entry at the Pump: Log odometer readings, fuel volume, and total cost in seconds via a lightweight dialog designed for one-handed operation. • Home Screen Widgets & Quick Settings: Add the Quick Settings Tile or Home Screen Glance Widget to record refuels instantly without opening the full application. • Fuel Economy Analytics: Automatically compute fuel consumption (MPG, L/100km, km/L, or MPG UK) alongside running costs per kilometer or mile. • Fully Offline Architecture: Never worry about weak cellular reception at remote fuel stations. All calculations and records run locally on your device. • Privacy-Centered Storage: Your driving and fueling logs remain strictly on your phone. TankPulse requires no account registration and contains zero analytics trackers. • Unrestricted Data Export: Backup your complete vehicle history anytime with standard CSV and spreadsheet exports directly to your local file storage. • Multi-Vehicle Management: Track individual logs, fuel expenses, and service milestones across multiple cars, motorcycles, or commercial fleet vehicles. • Metric and Imperial Support: Seamlessly switch between Liters, US Gallons, UK Gallons, Kilometers, and Miles with customizable currency formatting. Take control of your vehicle operating expenses with a streamlined, dependable fuel tracking tool.


Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
- 100% Offline: This application operates entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- No Accounts or Subscriptions: Enjoy complete utility with zero account creation, logins, or hidden recurring fees.
- Local Storage Only: All items, history, and preferences are retained strictly within on-device sandboxed storage.
