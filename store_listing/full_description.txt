LuxScale is an offline optical light meter, Plant PAR (PPFD & DLI) converter, and architectural workspace photometer powered by your phone's hardware ambient light sensor (Sensor.TYPE_LIGHT).

Designed for indoor growers, horticulturalists, photographers, cinematographers, and ergonomic workspace auditors, LuxScale eliminates paywalls and cloud subscriptions by delivering calibrated photometric telemetry in a fast, zero-permission native package.

KEY FEATURES:
• Live Hardware Photodiode Telemetry: Real-time SI Lux (lx) and Foot-Candles (fc) readouts with session Minimum, Peak, and Average tracking plus instant Hold lock.
• Sensor Calibration Multiplier: Apply a custom calibration factor (0.05× to 50.0×) to align your phone's front photodiode against a dedicated reference photometer or cosine diffuser.
• Horticultural PAR (PPFD & DLI) Engine: Convert calibrated Lux into Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density (µmol/m²/s) and Daily Light Integral (mol/m²/d) across selectable spectrum profiles (6500K Daylight, 3500K Warm White LED, and Full-Spectrum Horticultural Grow LED).
• Botanical & IES Target Benchmarks: Compare live canopy or desk light levels against IES office workspace standards (500 lx), shade foliage (2,000 lx), vegetative herb canopies (8,000 lx), and high-intensity fruiting arrays (20,000 lx).
• Cine & Photo Exposure Readout: Real-time EV100 exposure value calculation for incident scene lighting checks.
• Local SQLite Room Audit Log: Save, inspect, and reload multi-room or grow-shelf light audits completely offline.
• Daylight & Graphite Optical Lab Themes: Switch seamlessly between high-contrast Daylight Alabaster and Dark Graphite Optical Lab modes.
