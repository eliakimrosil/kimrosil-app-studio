DropScribe delivers fast, accurate, and completely private speech-to-text transcription directly on your Android device. Designed for seamless productivity, DropScribe allows you to convert voice messages and audio recordings into clear text without routing your private data through remote servers. Key Capabilities • Instant Share Sheet Integration: Transcribe voice notes directly from any messaging app. Tap share on any audio clip, select DropScribe, and view the transcription instantly in an overlay window without switching apps. • 100% On-Device Processing: Your audio never leaves your phone. All speech recognition models execute locally on your device hardware, safeguarding sensitive business discussions, medical records, and legal conversations. • Zero Account Requirement: Start transcribing immediately upon installation. No email registrations, no profile setup, and no mandatory cloud sign-ins. • Flexible Audio File Support: Transcribe standard audio formats including voice notes, lectures, meetings, and voice memos seamlessly. • Clean Export & Copy: Easily copy formatted text to your clipboard or export transcripts to notes and document editors with a single tap. • Privacy by Design: Built with zero background data collection and strict respect for device permissions. No audio files are ever uploaded, retained, or shared. Experience private, dependable transcription built specifically for Android power users who value security and speed.


Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
- 100% Offline: This application operates entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- No Accounts or Subscriptions: Enjoy complete utility with zero account creation, logins, or hidden recurring fees.
- Local Storage Only: All items, history, and preferences are retained strictly within on-device sandboxed storage.
