BrixScale is an offline specialty coffee brew ratio calculator, optical refractometer TDS extraction yield solver, and 4-stage pour-over pacer engineered for baristas, roasters, and home brewers.

KEY FEATURES:
• Live SCA Extraction Control Chart: Real-time 2D coordinate plotting of Extraction Yield (% EY) versus Strength (% TDS) with visual Gold Cup window (18.0%–22.0% EY) and brew ratio trajectory ray.
• Gravimetric Ratio & 4-Stage Pour Schedule: Instant water mass targets for V60 1:16, Chemex 1:17, AeroPress 1:13, and 9-Bar Espresso 1:2.0 with automatic Degas Bloom, Sweet Acidity, Spiral Body, and Final Drawdown gram milestones.
• Refractometer TDS & Brix Lab: Convert between % Total Dissolved Solids and °Brix, account for spent coffee grounds liquid retention (mL/g), calculate dissolved solubles mass, and compute exact bypass dilution water.
• Local SQLite Roast & Brew Log: Save, reload, and export your best specialty coffee dial-in recipes completely offline with zero cloud accounts, zero ads, and zero telemetry trackers.
