Never miss a store return deadline again. ReturnGuard helps you organize your purchases, store receipts securely on your device, and track return windows with clear visual countdowns. Every retailer has different return policies, ranging from 14 to 90 days. Misplaced receipts and forgotten dates often lead to wasted money on items you cannot use. ReturnGuard provides a reliable, private system to keep all your return windows organized in one central place. Key Features: - Active Return Countdown: View all pending returns sorted by urgency. Circular countdown meters show exactly how many days remain before deadlines expire. - Receipt Archival: Capture photos of paper receipts or import digital invoice screenshots. Keep proof of purchase ready whenever needed. - Cashier Return Mode: Instantly bring up receipt barcodes on full screen with one tap for smooth customer service counter processing. - Custom Store Policies: Easily set custom return durations to match store policies, seasonal extensions, or membership benefits. - Privacy First and Offline: Your receipts and purchase data remain stored strictly on your local device. No account creation required, no external server uploads, and zero tracking. Designed for clothing shoppers, gift recipients, and busy households seeking an organized way to manage purchases and protect their budgets.
