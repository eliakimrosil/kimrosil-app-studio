FlangeMatch provides essential piping, flange, and bolt specification data directly on your device, engineered specifically for field engineers, pipefitters, and boilermakers operating in remote or low-connectivity environments. Built for harsh industrial settings such as vessel interiors, fabrication shops, and offshore platforms, FlangeMatch operates completely offline. You get instant access to standard dimensional charts and bolting data without cloud logins, network connectivity, or intrusive interruptions. Core Features & Capabilities: • Comprehensive Flange Data: Rapidly look up nominal pipe sizes, pressure classes, outside diameters, bolt circle diameters, and facing dimensions. • Bolting & Torque Tables: Access required bolt counts, stud diameters, lengths, wrench sizes, and standard torque reference patterns. • Instant Offline Search: Powered by an optimized on-device search index for sub-millisecond retrieval of piping specifications. • Zero Network Dependency: 100% functional without cellular reception or Wi-Fi, preventing field lockouts in shielded refinery units. • Clean Professional Interface: High-contrast layout designed for quick reading and single-handed operation on the job site. • Privacy and Battery Optimized: Zero background data usage, zero analytics tracking, and no external account requirements. Eliminate cumbersome paper charts and unreliable connections. FlangeMatch delivers precise, standardized industrial piping specifications directly to your pocket whenever and wherever you need them.
