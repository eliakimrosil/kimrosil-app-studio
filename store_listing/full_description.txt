VatCal PH is an offline tax and invoice calculation utility for Filipino freelancers, small business owners, bookkeepers, and consumers. Quickly compute 12% Value-Added Tax (VAT), net vatable sales, Senior Citizen / PWD statutory discounts, and Creditable Withholding Tax (EWT) on your device.

Key Features:
• VAT Inclusive & Exclusive Modes: Enter gross or net amounts to calculate 12% Output VAT and Vatable Sales in real time.
• Senior Citizen & PWD 20% Discount: Compute VAT-exempt sales and the 20% statutory discount under RA 9994 and RA 10754.
• Creditable Withholding Tax (EWT): Apply standard 1%, 2%, or 5% withholding tax rates to determine net payable or receivable amounts.
• Built-In Arithmetic Keypad: Add or subtract line items directly inside the calculator without switching apps.
• One-Tap Invoice Breakdown Copy: Copy formatted calculation summaries to your clipboard for bookkeeping notes, messaging, or spreadsheets.
• 100% Offline & Private: Operates completely offline with zero advertisements, zero tracking, and no account registration.

Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
• 100% Offline: Operates entirely on your device without requiring an internet connection (`INTERNET` permission is not requested).
• Zero Data Collection: Does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
• Local Storage Only: User preferences (such as theme and default tax mode) are stored strictly in local device preferences.

Official Sources of Public Tax Information:
The mathematical formulas used in this calculator are based on publicly available Philippine tax laws and revenue regulations published by the following official government sources:
• Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) — Value-Added Tax (12% VAT) & Expanded Withholding Tax (EWT): https://www.bir.gov.ph/
• Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines — Republic Act No. 9994 (Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010) & Republic Act No. 10754 (Act Expanding the Benefits and Privileges of Persons with Disability): https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/
