DeciGauge is a precision acoustic sound pressure level (SPL) meter and occupational noise dose analyzer designed for acoustic engineers, industrial safety officers, audio technicians, and workplace safety compliance.

REAL-TIME DECIBEL MEASUREMENT & TELEMETRY
• High-performance 44.1 kHz PCM microphone sampling engine with minimal latency.
• Real-time digital readouts for instantaneous Sound Pressure Level (SPL), equivalent continuous sound level (Leq), maximum peak (Lmax), and minimum baseline (Lmin).
• High-precision laboratory HUD canvas featuring an analog needle gauge, dynamic peak hold tick, and real-time 8-band audio frequency spectrum visualizer.

FREQUENCY WEIGHTING FILTERS (IEC 61672-1)
• dBA (A-Weighting): Simulates the human ear response curve (40-phon Fletcher-Munson contour). Recommended for occupational noise regulation, workplace surveys, and environmental nuisance complaints.
• dBC (C-Weighting): Flat low-frequency response with sharp roll-off. Ideal for assessing low-end rumble, heavy machinery vibration, engine exhaust, and concert audio systems.
• dBZ (Z-Weighting): Flat, unweighted spectral measurement across the audible spectrum (20 Hz to 20,000 Hz).

STANDARDS-COMPLIANT TIME BALLISTICS
• Fast Response (125 ms): Standard for monitoring fluctuating, speech, and environmental noise levels.
• Slow Response (1,000 ms): Damped response for measuring steady-state background or factory noise.
• Impulse Response (35 ms rise / 1,500 ms decay): Captures rapid transient impacts, jackhammers, firearms, and machinery stamping.

OCCUPATIONAL NOSE DOSE MONITORING (NIOSH & OSHA)
• NIOSH REL Standard: 85 dBA criterion baseline with a 3 dB exchange rate.
• OSHA PEL Standard: 90 dBA criterion baseline with a 5 dB exchange rate.
• Computes real-time accumulated noise dose percentage, 8-hour projected Time-Weighted Average (TWA), and remaining permissible exposure time before reaching hazardous thresholds.
• Dynamic color-coded risk status badges: Safe, Caution, Action Required, and Immediate Hazard.

SESSION LOGGING & REPOSITORY
• Save noise exposure monitoring runs directly to an offline SQLite database.
• Review historical sessions with duration, Leq, Lmax, dose percentage, and weighting configuration.
• One-tap export to clipboard for safety compliance reporting and site audits.
