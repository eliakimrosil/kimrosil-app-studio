TorquePitch is a dedicated, 100% offline fastener torque calculator and thread specification assistant for mechanics, engine builders, machinists, and fabricators.

Calculate exact target bolt torque in ft-lb, Nm, and in-lb based on fastener diameter, thread pitch, material strength grade, and thread lubricant K-factor.

KEY CAPABILITIES

1. Fastener Torque & Clamp Preload Calculator
• Metric (ISO 898-1): M3 through M36 diameter across Class 4.8, 8.8, 10.9, 12.9, and Stainless A2-70.
• Imperial (SAE J429): #4 through 1-1/2" diameter across Grade 2, Grade 5, Grade 8, and Stainless 18-8.
• Coarse (UNC / standard) and Fine (UNF) thread pitch selection.
• Thread Lubricant K-Factor adjustment: Dry / Zinc Plated (K=0.20), Light Engine Oil (K=0.15), Anti-Seize (K=0.12), Moly Assembly Lube (K=0.10), and Threadlocker (K=0.16).
• Dynamic proof yield safety preload percentage adjustment (default 75% proof load).
• Instant stacked readout: Primary target torque, secondary unit conversions, clamping force in kN and lbs, and tensile stress area.

2. Tap Drill & Clearance Hole Spec Sheet
• Steel and cast iron tap drill size for standard 75% thread engagement.
• Aluminum and soft alloy tap drill size for 65% thread engagement to eliminate tap seizure.
• Fractional inch and decimal drill bit equivalents.
• Normal fit (free) and close fit (precision) through-hole clearance diameters.
• Tabular thread geometry: Major diameter, pitch, pitch diameter, and minor diameter.

3. Multi-Stage TTY & Tightening Sequence Tracker
• Torque-to-Yield (TTY) multi-stage angle torque setup: Snug torque plus Stage 2 and Stage 3 angular turns.
• Cylinder Head Spiral and Star/Cross pattern tightening guides.
• Interactive 1-to-10 fastener completion tracker with tactile haptic feedback.
