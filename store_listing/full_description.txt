BendSpec is a professional, 100% offline sheet metal forming calculator and flat pattern developed blank layout utility engineered for press brake operators, CNC fabricators, and mechanical engineers.

Eliminate trial-and-error scrap on the press brake. BendSpec computes precise neutral-axis Bend Allowance (BA), Outside Setback (OSB), and Bend Deduction (BD), plus recommended air bend V-die openings, minimum flange heights, and required press brake forming tonnage.

KEY CAPABILITIES

1. Precision Bend Mechanics (BA, BD, OSB)
• Enter material thickness (T), inside bend radius (R), and bend angle (deg).
• Choose standard alloys with validated K-factors or input custom neutral axis ratios:
  - Mild Steel 1008/1018 (K = 0.40, UTS = 450 MPa)
  - Stainless Steel 304/316 (K = 0.45, UTS = 650 MPa)
  - Aluminum 5052-H32 / 6061-T6 (K = 0.42, UTS = 230 MPa)
  - Structural Steel A36 (K = 0.42, UTS = 400 MPa)
  - Brass / Copper Half-Hard (K = 0.38, UTS = 300 MPa)
• Instant calculation of Bend Allowance, Outside Setback, and Bend Deduction down to 0.01 mm / 0.001 in.

2. V-Die Tooling & Press Brake Tonnage
• Automatic V-die width recommendation (6x, 8x, 10x, or 12x material thickness).
• Minimum safe flange length calculation to prevent sheets slipping into the V-die opening.
• Forming tonnage per meter and total press load computation based on material ultimate tensile strength (UTS), preventing machine overload.

3. Multi-Flange Flat Pattern Blank Layout
• Sequential flange ladder builder: add sequential flange leg lengths and bend deductions.
• Computes exact total developed blank length: Flat Length = Sum(Flanges) - Sum(Bend Deductions).
• Formatted multi-flange sequence export to clipboard for immediate machine shop communication.

4. Offline Job Ledger
• Save and catalog forming setups by part number, job title, and material spec.
• Instant recall of verified shop setups for repeat production runs.
