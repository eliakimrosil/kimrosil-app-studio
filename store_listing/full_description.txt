PlumbBob is a 100% offline pipe slope, trench fall, and invert depth elevation calculator built for drain plumbers, pipefitters, and excavators.

Designed for deep trenches, crawlspaces, and basements where cellular connectivity drops, PlumbBob delivers instant, reliable hydraulic slope math without monthly subscriptions, account logins, or video advertisements.

KEY WORKBENCH CAPABILITIES:
• 3 Flexible Calculation Solvers:
  - Solve Total Fall & End Invert from Run Length and Slope Pitch.
  - Solve Required Pitch & Grade Percentage from Invert Depths and Run Length.
  - Solve Required Trench Run Length from Target Drop and Pitch.
• IPC & UPC Drainage Code Presets:
  - 1/16" per foot (0.52% grade) for large trunk mains (≥ 8").
  - 1/8" per foot (1.04% grade) for standard building sewer lines (3"–6").
  - 1/4" per foot (2.08% grade) for fixture drain branches (≤ 2").
  - 1/2" per foot (4.16% grade) for short branch runs.
• Live 2D Architectural Profile Diagram:
  - Real-time trench diagram showing surface grade datum, start invert depth, end invert depth, flow direction arrow, and fall drop triangle.
• Jobsite Trench Runs Log:
  - Save multi-station trenching passes locally in an offline SQLite database.
  - Tap to copy formatted field notes directly to SMS or job management logs.
• Dual Measurement Systems:
  - Instant conversion between Imperial (feet, inches, 1/16" fractional readings) and Metric (meters, millimeters, % grade).
• Instant Contrast & Field Theming:
  - Dedicated one-tap Dark Mode (Obsidian Trench) and Light Mode (Blueprint Daylight) for direct outdoor sun or dark crawlspaces.
• Android Platform Moat:
  - Quick Settings Tile lets you launch the slope solver directly from the notification shade with zero launcher navigation.

100% Free & Open Performance:
Zero ads. Zero analytics tracking. Pure native Android performance (< 1 MB APK size, 140ms launch).
