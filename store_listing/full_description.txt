DipLink is a high-precision, 100% offline fuel storage tank calibration calculator designed for fuel station operators, transport depot managers, and petroleum logistics technicians.

DipLink converts physical dipstick depth readings (centimeters) into exact liquid volume (liters) using calibrated empirical tank lookup tables and real-time piecewise linear interpolation.

SUPPORTED STORAGE TANKS:
• Phoenix Diesel Tank: Cylindrical horizontal shell, 2.42 m diameter, 4.86 m length, max depth 242 cm, 22,354 Liters nominal capacity.
• Premium Tank (91 & 95 Unleaded): Cylindrical horizontal shell, 2.03 m diameter, 4.88 m length, max depth 203 cm, 16,000 Liters nominal capacity.

KEY FEATURES:
• Empirical Calibration Lookup: Real-time linear interpolation across 242-point Phoenix and 203-point Premium tank calibration charts.
• Hero Volume HUD: Instant readout of current liquid volume, fill percentage, and remaining ullage headroom.
• Visual Capacity Gauge: Real-time fluid meniscus level bar reflecting exact tank filling.
• Tactile Micro-Steppers: Quick-adjust depth buttons (-10, -5, -1, +1, +5, +10 cm) and preset capacity chips (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%).
• Tabular Telemetry Card: Comprehensive 2-column specifications display showing tank shell dimensions, nominal capacity, and calibration source.
• 100% Offline & Private: Zero network permissions, zero tracking, zero advertising, and zero third-party telemetry.
• Day / Night / Auto Theming: Carefully calibrated contrast modes for bright daylight forecourt inspections and nocturnal depot readings.
