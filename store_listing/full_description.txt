Gluewedge delivers precise mechanical calculations and visual clamp layout planning for woodworkers, cabinetmakers, and furniture builders. Achieving consistent edge joints requires balancing timber species density, clamping force, and glue line pressure. Gluewedge eliminates guesswork by converting panel dimensions and timber properties into exact clamp spacing, force requirements, and assembly countdowns. ENGINEERING-GRADE CALCULATIONS - Species-Specific Target Pressure: Reference recommended clamping pressures (PSI) across softwoods, medium hardwoods, and dense hardwoods to prevent glue starvation or dry joints. - Clamping Force Requirements: Input board length, width, and thickness to calculate total required clamping force (lbf) and load distribution per clamp. - 45-Degree Pressure Cone Visualization: Inspect dynamic visual cone overlays to ensure pressure zones overlap across the entire glue line, highlighting unpressured gaps before applying adhesive. - Clamp Spacing & Stagger Guidance: Determine optimal edge-to-edge spacing and alternate top/bottom clamp placement to prevent panel bowing. OPEN TIME & CURE TIMER - Countdown Timer: Track open assembly time and closed clamp duration based on adhesive type and ambient workshop conditions. - Audible & Haptic Alerts: Receive clear workshop-friendly alerts when assembly time is expiring or cure threshold is reached. SESSION ARCHIVE & LOGGING - Local History Log: Store panel dimensions, species, clamp count, and force metrics directly on device for production repeatability. - One-Tap Metric & Imperial Conversion: Seamlessly switch between inches, millimeters, pounds-force, and bar.
