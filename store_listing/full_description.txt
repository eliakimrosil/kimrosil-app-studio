KR Habit is a bespoke, offline-first daily habit and routine engine engineered for lasting behavioral momentum.

BUILD GENUINE HABIT AUTOMATICITY
Unlike simplistic streak trackers that punish you completely for missing a single day due to travel or illness, KR Habit calculates a scientifically grounded Habit Strength score (0% to 100%) using exponential smoothing. Every consistent day builds compounding momentum, and occasional misses cause only graceful decay rather than wiping out weeks of hard work.

KEY FEATURES
• Exponential Habit Strength Scoring: Measure real habit automaticity, not just fragile streaks.
• Visual Consistency Heatmap: View your last 60+ days of progress in an interactive multi-week matrix.
• Tactile Haptic Check-Ins: Feel satisfying physical haptic feedback with every check-in.
• Google Account & Cloud Sync: Sign in with your Google account to backup and synchronize habits with Cloud Firestore across multiple devices.
• 100% Offline-First Architecture: Your habit ledger is always stored locally in SQLite with zero latency. Cloud sync operates unobtrusively in the background.
• Working Day & Night Theming: Handcrafted Emerald Obsidian aesthetic with dark, light, and automatic system modes.
• Ad-Free & Respectful: Zero ads, zero background telemetry tracking.
