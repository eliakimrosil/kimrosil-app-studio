FastArc is a lightweight, zero-bloat intermittent fasting timer built specifically for the 16:8 protocol. Designed with an offline-first philosophy, it requires zero account creation, displays zero advertisements, collects zero telemetry, and operates completely without an internet connection.

KEY FEATURES

• Zero-Bloat 16:8 Protocol Engine
Track your 16-hour fasting window and 8-hour eating window with precision. Clear elapsed time, remaining countdown, and projected finish targets updated in real time.

• Real-Time Metabolic Stage Telemetry
Understand what happens inside your body as your fast progresses through 4 distinct physiological stages:
1. Digestion & Blood Sugar Stabilization (0h – 4h)
2. Glycogen Depletion & Fat Mobilization (4h – 12h)
3. Ketosis & Accelerated Fat Oxidation (12h – 16h)
4. Autophagy Threshold & Cellular Cleanup (16h+)

• Lock-Screen & Home-Screen Keyguard Widget
Check your fasting progress without unlocking your device. FastArc includes a native Android AppWidget compatible with both home screens and keyguard lock screens, displaying active fasting status, elapsed time, and metabolic stage at a glance.

• Public Lock-Screen Notification Live Tile
Optional silent, ongoing notification tile with Android Public Visibility lets you view your live fasting countdown and log water intake directly from your lock screen.

• One-Tap Quick Start Adjustment
Forgot to tap start right after dinner? Adjust your start time backward in one tap (-15m, -30m, -1h, -2h, or custom time) without resetting your history.

• Hydration Logger
Quickly log water intake in 250ml intervals to maintain optimal hydration during fasting windows.

• Working Dark, Light & Auto Themes
Built with athletic typography, high-contrast visual telemetry, and dynamic themes designed for instant readability indoors, outdoors, or in pitch darkness.

• True Native Android Performance
Written cleanly in pure native Android framework code. Under 1 MB total download size, instant cold start in under 50 milliseconds, and zero background battery drain.
