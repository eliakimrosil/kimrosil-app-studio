Power Key Assistant Bridge provides seamless hardware button remapping, enabling you to launch your favorite virtual assistant, digital companion, or utility shortcut directly via your device power key. Many devices lock hardware shortcut keys to proprietary assistant software or system search engines. Power Key Assistant Bridge acts as an intelligent interceptor service, capturing hardware key events and seamlessly redirecting the intent to your chosen application without latency or interruption. Key Features: - Instant Hardware Interception: Detects power key events and redirects execution instantly to your preferred assistant or shortcut. - Custom Target Selection: Configure any installed assistant, automation tool, or frequently used app as the primary trigger. - Offline-First Architecture: Operates entirely locally on your device with no remote servers or external dependencies. - Privacy Focused: Zero network tracking, zero analytics SDKs, and zero telemetry collection. - Lightweight and Efficient: Optimized background listener designed to consume negligible battery and system memory. - No Subscriptions: All utility features are available out of the box with no account creation or recurring fees required. Permissions and Accessibility Disclosure: This app utilizes the Android AccessibilityService API to detect hardware key presses when the screen is active and route them to your configured assistant. The Accessibility Service is strictly used for button detection and intent dispatch. It does not monitor screen contents, collect personal data, or transmit keystrokes. Take full control of your hardware shortcuts with a lightweight, secure utility designed to personalize your everyday workflow.
