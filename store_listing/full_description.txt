ClampPitch is a dedicated, 100% offline workbench calculator and assembly assistant for woodworkers, cabinetmakers, and furniture builders.

Stop guessing clamp layout during glue-ups. ClampPitch calculates exact center-to-center clamp spacing, total clamping pressure, and edge line PSI based on timber species Janka hardness and clamp mechanics.

KEY CAPABILITIES

1. Glueline Spacing & Pressure Calculator
• Select timber species: Hard Maple, White Oak, Black Walnut, Cherry, Ash, Pine, or Teak.
• Enter panel length and board thickness.
• Choose clamp hardware: Parallel / K-Body (1,000 lbs force), Heavy Pipe Clamp (1,200 lbs force), or Standard F-Style Clamp (600 lbs force).
• Computes required clamp count, center-to-center pitch distance, and average joint PSI.
• Highlights 45-degree pressure cone coverage to prevent starving joints or leaving uncompressed edge dead zones.

2. Adhesive Open-Time Countdown Timer
• Built-in cure curves for common workshop glues: Titebond Original, Titebond II, Titebond III, Liquid Hide Glue, Polyurethane, and 5-Minute Epoxy.
• Ambient temperature slider dynamically adjusts open-assembly limits based on shop heat.
• Tactile one-tap countdown timer alerts you before adhesive skins over.

3. Cabinet & Frame Case Squarer
• Enter diagonal measurements (Corner A-D vs Corner B-C).
• Instantly calculates diagonal differential, out-of-square gap, and the exact racking angle to pull cases perfectly square.
