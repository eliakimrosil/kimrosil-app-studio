VatCal PH is an offline invoice and tax calculation utility for freelancers, small business owners, bookkeepers, and consumers. Quickly compute 12% Value-Added Tax (VAT), net vatable sales, 20% Senior/PWD discounts, and Withholding Tax (1%, 2%, or 5%) on your device.

Key Features:
• VAT Inclusive & Exclusive Modes: Enter gross or net amounts to calculate 12% VAT and Vatable Sales in real time.
• Senior & PWD 20% Discount: Compute VAT-exempt sales and the 20% discount with a single toggle.
• Withholding Tax (EWT): Apply standard 1%, 2%, or 5% withholding tax rates to determine net payable or receivable amounts.
• Built-In Arithmetic Keypad: Add or subtract line items directly inside the calculator without switching apps.
• One-Tap Invoice Breakdown Copy: Copy formatted calculation summaries to your clipboard for bookkeeping notes, messaging, or spreadsheets.
• 100% Offline & Private: Operates completely offline with zero advertisements, zero tracking, and no account registration.

Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
• 100% Offline: Operates entirely on your device without requiring an internet connection.
• Zero Data Collection: Does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
• Local Storage Only: User preferences (such as theme and default calculation mode) are stored strictly in local device preferences.
