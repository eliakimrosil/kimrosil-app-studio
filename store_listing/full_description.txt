IronPulse is an offline strength training and workout log designed for focused lifters. Built for speed and reliability, it removes unnecessary clutter, forced cloud accounts, and intrusive interruptions so you can focus entirely on your training session. Whether you train in a commercial gym, garage gym, or basement with limited reception, IronPulse operates with zero network dependencies. All workout data is stored locally on your device for immediate access and complete privacy. Structured Training Programs Select from established lifting templates or tailor a routine to match your schedule: • Push Pull Legs (PPL) • Upper / Lower Split • 5x5 Strength Routine • Custom Routine Builder Efficient Workout Logging • Clean set, rep, and weight entry without friction • Progressive overload tracking cues based on previous performances • Integrated rest timer with visual alerts and haptic vibration feedback • Quick adjustments between warm-up and working sets Performance History & Volume Tracking • Comprehensive log vault documenting completed workouts • Calculation of total volume lifted, sets completed, and session duration • Review past training sessions to measure strength progression over time Designed for Focus • Complete offline utility: No internet connection required to log workouts • Zero registration: No email signups, passwords, or personal profiles needed • Private: Your workout history remains stored on your local device


Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
- 100% Offline: This application operates entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- No Accounts or Subscriptions: Enjoy complete utility with zero account creation, logins, or hidden recurring fees.
- Local Storage Only: All items, history, and preferences are retained strictly within on-device sandboxed storage.
