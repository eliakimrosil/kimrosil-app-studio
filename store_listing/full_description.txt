Never lose track of your borrowed tools, books, or outdoor gear again. WhoHasMy is a dedicated personal lending organizer designed for homeowners, hobbyists, DIY enthusiasts, and friendly neighbors who frequently share equipment. When you lend out a power drill, lawnmower, board game, or camping tent, it is easy to forget who borrowed it months later. WhoHasMy provides a simple, structured vault to record every lent item immediately, helping you protect your valuable gear without awkward social friction. Key Features: • Item Vault: Log items with photos, borrower names, lending dates, and expected return dates for fast visual reference. • Friendly Reminders: Generate considerate check-in messages with a single tap to follow up easily without uncomfortable confrontation. • Quick Return Archive: Mark returned gear instantly to maintain a clean dashboard and keep a complete historical log of past loans. • Privacy Focused: Your records remain private on your device. We do not sell your personal data or track your personal lending activities. • Fast and Lightweight: Minimalist design built for quick entry so you can log items in seconds right when handing them over. Whether sharing workshop machinery with family or lending novels to friends, WhoHasMy keeps your belongings organized and accounted for over time.


Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
- 100% Offline: This application operates entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- No Accounts or Subscriptions: Enjoy complete utility with zero account creation, logins, or hidden recurring fees.
- Local Storage Only: All items, history, and preferences are retained strictly within on-device sandboxed storage.
