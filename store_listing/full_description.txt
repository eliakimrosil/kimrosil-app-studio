BrazeRing is a 100% offline silver alloy consumption, preform ring sizing, and nitrogen purge flow rate calculator engineered for HVAC/R service mechanics, pipefitters, and refrigeration engineers.

Designed for machine rooms, rooftop condensing units, and walk-in freezer basements with zero cellular reception, BrazeRing eliminates expensive silver alloy waste, guessing preform wire gauges, and joint scale oxidation.

KEY WORKBENCH CAPABILITIES:
• Precise Capillary Annular Sizing:
  - Calculates exact joint annular volume for standard ACR copper tubing (1/4 in through 2-1/8 in OD).
  - Automatically incorporates AWS/CDA 25% meniscus and fillet cap reserve.
• Multi-Alloy Density Engine:
  - BCuP-2 (0% Silver phosphor copper).
  - BCuP-5 (Sil-Fos 15% Silver high-ductility alloy).
  - BAg-36 (Safety-Silv 45% Silver cadmium-free alloy).
  - BAg-50 (50% High-Silver specialty alloy).
• Wire & Preform Ring Solvers:
  - Converts required joint volume into linear inches of standard 1/16 in and 1/8 in braze rod.
  - Computes exact drop preform ring wire gauge (20 AWG to 1/8 in) for internal socket pre-placement.
• Inert Nitrogen Sweep Calculator:
  - Determines recommended low-pressure nitrogen sweep flow (SCFH) and 1-2 PSI regulator benchmarks to prevent black copper oxide scale without cooling the joint.
• Live 2D Architectural Joint Cross-Section:
  - Real-time hardware canvas rendering tube insertion, capillary clearance channel, alloy meniscus fillet, and heat target zone.
• Jobsite Multi-Joint Run Log:
  - Record multi-joint installation jobs into local offline SQLite storage.
  - Single-tap clipboard export to send alloy requisitions to the supply house.
• Fast Contrast & Field Theming:
  - Quick header switcher for Deep Obsidian Forge (Dark) and Daylight Blueprint (Light) modes.
• Android Platform Moat:
  - Quick Settings Tile lets you launch the braze calculator instantly from your notification shade.

Zero ads. Zero tracking. Ultra-fast native Android performance (< 110 KB APK, 120ms cold launch).
