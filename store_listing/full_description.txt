PitchGrid is a bespoke studio-grade audio frequency generator and musical microtonal workbench crafted for sound designers, acoustic engineers, synth enthusiasts, and musicians.

Engineered with a pure native Android 44.1 kHz PCM audio synthesis engine, PitchGrid produces mathematically pure waveforms in real time with zero latency, zero audible popping, and zero cloud dependencies.
