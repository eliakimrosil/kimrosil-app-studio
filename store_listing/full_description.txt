SolderWick is an offline reference toolkit and calculator built for electronics engineers, hardware technicians, PCB designers, and hobbyists at the workbench. Engineered for RF-shielded environments, remote field repair, and clean hardware labs, SolderWick functions entirely without network connectivity. Access physical reference tables, formulas, and pinout diagrams immediately without telemetry, accounts, or recurring subscriptions. CORE WORKBENCH TOOLS • SMD Marking & Package Decoder: Identify semiconductor marking codes, resistor EIA-96 standards, diode footprints, and IC packaging. • Thermal Dissipation Calculators: Calculate junction temperature, heatsink thermal resistance (Rth), and trace temperature rise under load. • PCB Trace Width & Impedance: IPC-2152 current capacity limits, microstrip, and coplanar waveguide characteristic impedance analysis. • Passive Component Tooling: Color code decoders for 4, 5, and 6-band resistors, ceramic capacitor codes, and inductor reactance calculators. • Power & Battery Metrics: Ohm's law relationships, voltage divider ratios, LED series dropping resistance, and battery runtime estimation. • Live Chronometer Integration: Monitor solder paste cure times and reflow soaking cycles directly through system status chronometers. BUILT FOR INTEGRITY & PRIVACY • True Offline Architecture: Complete functionality without internet permissions or cloud synchronization. • Zero Accounts Required: No registration, authentication prompts, or cloud profiles. • Predictable Reference Data: Standardized formulas and industry tables available instantly during assembly and diagnostics.
