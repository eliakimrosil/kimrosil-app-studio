StairRise is an offline engineering and carpentry calculator designed for contractors, architects, carpenters, and DIY builders. Quickly calculate precise dimensions for straight, L-shaped, and U-shaped staircases while ensuring compliance with standard building codes (IBC/IRC). Eliminate manual layout errors and job-site recalculations. Enter your total rise, target riser height, or tread depth to instantly generate comprehensive dimensional schedules. Key Features: • Dimensional Calculation: Accurate riser height, tread run, stringer throat depth, total run, and incline angle. • Building Code Validation: Automated warnings when riser heights, tread depths, or angles exceed IBC/IRC residential and commercial safety tolerances. • Headroom Clearance Tracking: Calculate minimum vertical headroom along the stair slope to prevent ceiling clearance conflicts. • Stringer Layout Dimensions: Get exact layout points for cutting wood, steel, or concrete stringers without cumulative marking errors. • Fractional & Metric Units: Seamlessly switch between imperial fractions (down to 1/16 inch) and metric millimeters. • Material Estimation: Calculate required stringer lengths, tread counts, and riser board materials for accurate job costing. • Project Save & Export: Store job-site measurements locally and export formatted dimension summaries for blueprints or crew handoffs. • Fully Offline & Private: No internet connection required, no user accounts, no subscriptions, and zero trackers or data collection.
