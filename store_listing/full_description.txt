KerfNotch is a dedicated cut layout and joinery calculator designed for carpenters, woodworkers, and timber framers who require accurate measurements directly on the workshop floor. Accurate cutting calculations require accounting for blade thickness and material removal. KerfNotch calculates kerf offsets, dado spacing, lap joints, and repeated notch patterns with exact decimal and fractional dimensions. Designed to operate completely offline, the application ensures uninterrupted workflow in basement shops, remote job sites, and low-connectivity environments. Key Features: - Kerf Offset Calculation: Accurately compensate for table saw, miter saw, and circular saw blade widths to prevent undersized stock. - Notch and Dado Spacing: Compute equal-spaced slots, half-lap joints, and box joint cut sequences with consistent spacing. - Quick Settings Integration: Access your cut calculations immediately through the Android Quick Settings tile without interrupting shop tasks. - Complete Offline Reliability: Operates without internet connectivity, cloud authentication, or external account requirements. - State Preservation: Active measurements and cut lists remain preserved when switching between tools or backgrounding the application. - Zero Telemetry: Completely private with no background analytics tracking, background network activity, or unnecessary system permissions. Built for trade professionals and serious woodworkers seeking a dependable layout tool that performs reliably under demanding shop conditions.
