ConduitFill is a precision electrical conduit capacity, conductor jam ratio, and pull box sizing calculator built strictly to National Electrical Code (NEC) Chapter 9 standards for electricians, electrical engineers, inspectors, and contractors.

NEC CHAPTER 9 TABLE 1 FILL CAPACITIES
• Automatically enforces standard allowable percentage fill limits:
  - 1 Conductor: 53% allowable fill area.
  - 2 Conductors: 31% allowable fill area.
  - 3 or More Conductors: 40% allowable fill area.
  - 60% Nipple Fill Exception: Toggle for short raceways not exceeding 24 inches (600 mm).

OFFICIAL NEC TABLE 4 RACEWAY TYPES & SIZES
• Electrical Metallic Tubing (EMT)
• PVC Schedule 40 & PVC Schedule 80
• Rigid Metal Conduit (RMC)
• Flexible Metal Conduit (FMC)
• Trade sizes supported: 1/2", 3/4", 1", 1-1/4", 1-1/2", 2", 2-1/2", 3", 3-1/2", and 4".

OFFICIAL NEC TABLE 5 CONDUCTOR TYPES & GAUGES
• Insulation types: THHN / THWN / THWN-2, XHHW / XHHW-2, and RHW / USE.
• Wire gauges: 14 AWG, 12 AWG, 10 AWG, 8 AWG, 6 AWG, 4 AWG, 3 AWG, 2 AWG, 1 AWG, 1/0, 2/0, 3/0, 4/0, 250 kcmil, 300 kcmil, 350 kcmil, 400 kcmil, and 500 kcmil.
• Mix and match multiple different conductor gauges and quantities within the same conduit run.

CONDUCTOR JAM RATIO SAFETY ENGINE
• Evaluates the ratio between conduit internal diameter and conductor outside diameter (D/d).
• Flags the critical jam danger zone between 2.8 and 3.2, where three conductors can wedge side-by-side during wire pulls and cause catastrophic cable insulation shearing.

NEC ARTICLE 314 PULL BOX SIZING
• Computes minimum required pull box dimensions:
  - Straight Pulls: 8 × largest conduit trade diameter.
  - Angle & U-Pulls: 6 × largest conduit diameter plus sum of remaining conduit diameters on the same wall.

• Real-time fill percentage indicator and status badge (Compliant, Caution, Code Violation).
