Overview & Core Capabilities

JF Gadget Repair is a purpose-built, 100% offline Android job manager engineered for mobile phone repair technicians and gadget service counters. Track every customer phone from walk-in receiving through bench diagnostics to final repaired release without cloud subscriptions, ads, or paper notebooks.

  • Fast Phone Receiving (Intake): Auto-generates sequential ticket numbers (JF-YYYYMMDD-001), records customer contact info, phone brand/model, IMEI/serial, privacy-masked lock passcode notes, accessories left, pre-existing condition flags, issue tags, deposit, and target release date.
  • Before & After Condition Photos: Capture or import condition proof photos before opening the phone and after completing the repair to protect your shop from disputes.
  • On-Screen Customer Signatures: Capture fingertip/stylus customer signatures at both intake and release, automatically embedded into generated PDF claim stubs and release receipts.
  • Complete Release & Payment Checkout: Log repaired or pull-out outcomes, post-repair QA verification checks, final price, parts cost, payment method (Cash, GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer, Card), claimant name, and warranty duration.
  • Printable & Shareable PDF Receipts: Generate crisp vector PDF Claim Stubs and Official Release Receipts ready for Android Print or 1-tap sharing via Messenger, WhatsApp, or SMS.
  • One-Tap Call & Ready-for-Pickup SMS: Contact customers directly with pre-filled status-aware SMS messages displaying ticket #, device model, and exact remaining balance.
  • Shop Dashboard, Warranty Tracker & JSON Backup: Monitor revenue, parts cost, net profit, receivables, overdue units, active warranty countdowns, Light/Dark themes, and portable JSON backups.

Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee: Operates 100% offline in sandboxed local SQLite storage with zero ads, zero tracking SDKs, and zero mandatory accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is JF Gadget Repair completely offline?

Yes. All repair tickets, condition photos, signatures, warranties, and shop settings are stored locally in on-device SQLite storage with zero server calls or cloud logins required.

Can I print or send Claim Stubs and Release Receipts to customers?

Yes. Tap Receipt on any ticket to preview, print via Android Print (Wi-Fi/LAN/Bluetooth print services), share a vector PDF via Messenger/WhatsApp/Email, or send as formatted SMS text.

Can I customize the shop name, currency, and warranty terms?

Yes. Open the Settings tab to configure your business name, address, phone number, ticket prefix, currency symbol (default ₱ PHP, customizable), default warranty period, Light/Dark theme, and JSON backup/restore.
