Overview & Core Capabilities

JF Gadget Repair is a purpose-built, offline-first Android shop manager engineered for mobile phone repair technicians and gadget service counters. Track every customer phone, spare part, and retail accessory from intake to release without ads, subscriptions, or paper notebooks.

  • Fast Phone Receiving (Intake): Auto-generates sequential ticket numbers (JF-YYYYMMDD-001), records customer contact info, phone brand/model, IMEI/serial, privacy-masked lock passcode notes, accessories left, pre-existing condition flags, issue tags, deposit, and target release date.
  • Parts & Retail Products Inventory: Manage repair parts (LCDs, batteries, charging ports, flex cables, ICs) and retail accessories (tempered glass, chargers, cables, cases) in the dedicated Stock tab with cost/sell margins, drawer/shelf bins, restock & adjustment logs, low-stock alerts, and 1-tap Walk-In Quick Sales.
  • Smart Repair Ticket Auto-Deduct: Pick parts directly from stock inside any ticket or release checkout (auto-ranked by matching device model) to atomically deduct inventory, update parts cost, preserve manual part notes, and restore stock automatically on undo.
  • Before & After Condition Photos: Capture or import local condition proof photos before opening the phone and after completing the repair to protect your shop from disputes.
  • On-Screen Customer Signatures: Capture fingertip/stylus customer signatures at both intake and release, automatically embedded into generated PDF claim stubs and release receipts.
  • Complete Release & Payment Checkout: Log repaired or pull-out outcomes, post-repair QA verification checks, final price, parts cost, payment method (Cash, GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer, Card), claimant name, and warranty duration.
  • Printable & Shareable PDF Receipts with Series Numbers: Generate crisp vector PDF Claim Stubs and Official Release Receipts stamped with automatic collision-proof sequential Series Numbers (customizable prefix & starting number, e.g. SR-000001) ready for Android Print or 1-tap sharing via Messenger, WhatsApp, or SMS.
  • Shop Dashboard, JSON Backup & Optional Google Cloud Sync: Monitor combined repair & retail revenue, net profit, collectibles, low-stock & overdue alerts, active warranties, Light/Dark themes, portable JSON backups, and optional Google Sign-In cross-device Firestore sync.

Data Safety & Privacy Guarantee: Works 100% offline in sandboxed local SQLite storage with zero ads and zero ad trackers. Google Sign-In & Cloud Firestore sync are strictly optional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does JF Gadget Repair work offline, and is Google Sign-In optional?

Yes. All repair tickets, inventory stock, photos, signatures, warranties, and shop settings work 100% offline in local SQLite storage with no account required. If you want multi-device backup, you can optionally sign in with Google in Settings or on the Jobs screen to sync with Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Can I print or send Claim Stubs and Release Receipts to customers?

Yes. Tap Receipt on any ticket to preview, print via Android Print (Wi-Fi/LAN/Bluetooth print services), share a vector PDF via Messenger/WhatsApp/Email, or send as formatted SMS text.

Can I customize the shop name, currency, and warranty terms?

Yes. Open the Settings tab to configure your business name, address, phone number, ticket prefix, currency symbol (default ₱ PHP, customizable), default warranty period, Light/Dark theme, and JSON backup/restore.
