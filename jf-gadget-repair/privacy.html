Privacy Policy for JF Gadget Repair

Last Updated: November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android mobile application JF Gadget Repair (the "Application"), developed and published by Eliakim Rosil ("we", "us", or "our") under the KimRosil App Studio developer portfolio (https://apps.kimrosil.com).

The Application is distributed via official APK releases and https://apps.kimrosil.com/jf-gadget-repair/.

1. Offline-First Architecture & Local Storage

The Application is engineered as an offline-first business utility for mobile phone repair technicians and gadget shops.

  • Works 100% Offline Without an Account: Full repair intake, parts & retail inventory management, walk-in sales, warranty tracking, PDF receipts, and portable JSON backups work completely offline with zero registration required.
  • Sandboxed Local Storage: All repair tickets, status timelines, inventory stock, movement logs, condition photos, customer signatures, and shop preferences are stored inside Android's sandboxed local app storage (SQLite database and app-private media directories).
  • User-Initiated Sharing & Backups Only: Claim Stub PDFs, Release Receipt PDFs, SMS messages, phone calls, and JSON backups are created or shared strictly when you explicitly tap Share, Print, Call, SMS, or Export Backup via Android system intents.
  • Data Deletion: Deleting records inside the app, clearing app storage via Android Settings, or uninstalling the Application permanently deletes local records and attached media.

2. Optional Google Sign-In & Firebase Cloud Firestore Sync

Cloud backup and multi-device synchronization are strictly optional and remain disabled until you explicitly choose Continue with Google inside the app.

  • Authentication Data: If you choose to sign in, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account identifier (UID), display name, and email address solely to identify your private cloud workspace.
  • Synced Business Data: When signed in, your repair tickets, status timelines, customer signatures, shop settings, inventory items, ticket part links, and stock movement logs are synchronized to your isolated Cloud Firestore path (jf_gadget_repair_users/{uid}/...) via Google Firebase so you can restore or access your shop records across your devices.
  • Device Condition Photos Stay Local: Before/after camera and gallery photos remain exclusively in local device storage and are not uploaded to Cloud Firestore.
  • Sign Out & Control: You may sign out at any time in Settings or the Account dialog; signing out stops all cloud sync while keeping all of your local offline data intact. To request deletion of synced cloud data, contact the developer at the email below.

3. Zero Advertising & Zero Ad Trackers

  • Zero Ads: We do not display advertisements or integrate ad networks.
  • No Data Selling or Profiling: We never sell, rent, or share user or customer shop data with third-party data brokers or advertisers.

4. Permissions & Device Access

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA, optional): Requested only when you tap Camera to photograph a customer's device condition before or after repair. Photos are saved exclusively in local app storage.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, optional usage): Used solely if you voluntarily sign in with Google to synchronize your own shop database with Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.
  • Dialer / SMS / Print / Storage Access Framework: Calling, SMS messaging, receipt printing, gallery selection, and JSON backup export/import use standard Android system intents and do not require intrusive contact, call-log, SMS-sending, or broad storage permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application is a professional repair shop management utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
