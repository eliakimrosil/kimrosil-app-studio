Privacy Policy for JF Gadget Repair

Last Updated: November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android mobile application JF Gadget Repair (the "Application"), developed and published by Eliakim Rosil ("we", "us", or "our") under the KimRosil App Studio developer portfolio (https://apps.kimrosil.com).

The Application is distributed via official APK releases and https://apps.kimrosil.com/jf-gadget-repair/.

1. 100% Offline-First Architecture & Sandboxed Local Storage

The Application is engineered strictly as an offline-first business utility for repair technicians.

  • Zero Developer Data Collection: We do NOT collect, harvest, transmit, or access any customer repair records, phone numbers, IMEI numbers, photos, signatures, or billing data entered by the shop owner.
  • Sandboxed Local Storage: All repair tickets, status timelines, condition photos, customer signatures, and shop preferences remain exclusively inside Android's sandboxed local app storage (local SQLite database and app-private media directories).
  • User-Initiated Sharing & Backups Only: Claim Stub PDFs, Release Receipt PDFs, SMS messages, phone calls, and JSON backups are created or shared strictly when you explicitly tap Share, Print, Call, SMS, or Export Backup via Android system intents.
  • Zero Internet Requirement: The Application operates 100% offline and does not request the INTERNET permission.
  • Data Deletion: Deleting tickets inside the app, clearing app storage via Android Settings, or uninstalling the Application permanently deletes local records and attached media.

2. Zero Advertising & Zero Third-Party SDKs

  • Zero Ads: We do not display advertisements.
  • Zero Trackers / Analytics: No analytics SDKs, ad networks, or crash telemetry frameworks are embedded in the APK.
  • No Accounts or Paywalls: Full functionality works immediately without registration or subscriptions.

3. Permissions & Device Access

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA, optional): Requested only when you tap Camera to photograph a customer's device condition before or after repair. Photos are saved exclusively in local app storage and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Dialer / SMS / Print / Storage Access Framework: Calling, SMS messaging, receipt printing, gallery selection, and JSON backup export/import use standard Android system intents and do not require intrusive contact, call-log, SMS-sending, or broad storage permissions.

4. Children's Privacy

The Application is a professional repair shop management utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal data from any user.
