Privacy Policy for FlowStep

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

KimRosil App Studio Company (Developer: Eliakim Rosil)

At KimRosil App Studio, user privacy is our foundational principle. FlowStep is designed from the ground up as a 100% offline-first application.

1. Zero Data Collection

FlowStep does not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information, device identifiers, location data, or usage telemetry.

2. Local Device Storage

All goals, micro-steps, stray thoughts, and lifetime momentum records are stored strictly in private SQLite databases on your local physical device. No data is synchronized with external servers or cloud providers.

3. Optional Cloud Features (BYOK)

If you voluntarily choose to enter a personal Google Gemini API key in Settings, API requests to decompose tasks are sent directly from your device to Google Generative Language APIs. KimRosil App Studio has zero access to your key or your requests. If no key is entered, FlowStep functions completely offline using built-in deterministic heuristic algorithms.

4. No Advertising or Third-Party SDKs

FlowStep contains no tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, advertising brokers, or third-party behavioral trackers.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, visit [https://apps.kimrosil.com/](https://apps.kimrosil.com/).
