BeltDeflect is an offline mechanical engineering and field technician utility designed for accurate V-belt, serpentine, and timing belt installation, deflection force measurement, and acoustic frequency tension gauging.

Proper power transmission belt tension prevents catastrophic sheave wear, premature bearing failure, slip heat generation, and fatigue breakage. BeltDeflect brings industrial-grade tension calculations and transverse acoustic resonance metering straight to your Android workbench—without requiring internet access or cloud accounts.

DUAL-ENGINE TENSION VERIFICATION:
1. ISO Deflection Distance Force Meter: Calculates standard 1/64" deflection per inch of span (Span / 64 mm) alongside exact recommended force limits for both New and Run-In (used) belts.
2. Transverse Acoustic Resonance Pluck Gauge: Measures belt vibration frequency in Hertz (Hz) using real-time audio microphone FFT (Fast Fourier Transform), calculating tension via T = 4 * m * L^2 * f^2.

FEATURES:
• Industrial Belt Profile Engine: Presets for Classical V-Belts (A, B, C), Narrow Wedge (3V, 5V), and Micro-V Multi-Rib (PK) with standard linear mass densities.
• Real-Time Pulley Schematic: Visual 2D canvas displaying sheave pitch diameters, center span geometry, contact wrap angle, and center deflection.
• Dedicated Acoustic Pluck Tuner: Full-screen HUD with high-contrast frequency dial, dominant harmonic peak tracking, ±1.5 Hz target tolerance indicator, and blind haptic confirmation pulse.
• Sheave Pitch Catalog: Built-in database of standard industrial sheave diameters for quick single-tap selection.
• Quick Settings Tile: Android Quick Settings tile support for instant field telemetry readouts right from your notification shade.
• Field Report Sharing: Export comprehensive machine inspection certificates and belt specifications via text sharing.
• Day / Night / Auto Adaptive Themes: High-contrast field-tested dark and light themes crafted for dimly lit machinery rooms and sunny outdoor plants alike.

100% OFFLINE WORKBENCH PRIVACY:
• Zero Internet Permission: Operates completely offline with zero telemetry, analytics, or background data transfer.
• Pure Native Architecture: Ultra-lightweight native Android application built for instant startup and low battery consumption.
