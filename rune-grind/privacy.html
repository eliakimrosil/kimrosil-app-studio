Privacy Policy for RuneGrind

Last updated: October 15, 2026

RuneGrind is developed by KimRosil App Studio as a free, offline native Android game.

Information Collection and Use

RuneGrind operates 100% offline on your device.

  • We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, telemetry, location data, or device identifiers.
  • All character progression, stats, items, cards, and game saves are stored locally on your device via an internal SQLite database (`runegrind.db`) and private SharedPreferences.
  • The app requires no internet access, displays zero advertisements, and includes zero third-party analytics SDKs.

Security

Because all data resides exclusively in local application sandbox storage on your device, your gameplay data remains private and under your direct control.
