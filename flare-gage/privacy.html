Privacy Policy for FlareGage Flare Dimension Tool

Last Updated: October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android mobile application FlareGage Flare Dimension Tool (the "Application"), developed and published by Eliakim Rosil ("we", "us", or "our") under the KimRosil App Studio developer portfolio (https://apps.kimrosil.com).

The Application is distributed via the Google Play Store, official APK releases, and https://apps.kimrosil.com/flare-gage/.

1. 100% Offline-First Architecture & Sandboxed Local Storage

The Application is engineered strictly as an offline-first mobile utility.

  • Zero Personal Information: We do NOT collect, harvest, store, or transmit personal identity details (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, device identifiers, or location data) to any proprietary developer servers or external third parties.
  • Sandboxed Local Storage: Any user preferences, search parameters, or custom inputs are stored strictly on your local device within Android's sandboxed storage (`SharedPreferences`, Room, or local SQLite database). Your personal application records never leave your physical device.
  • Zero Internet Requirement: The Application operates entirely on-device and makes zero outbound network requests.
  • Data Deletion: Uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage via Android Settings (`Settings > Apps > FlareGage Flare Dimension Tool > Storage > Clear Data`) permanently and irreversibly deletes all locally saved records.

2. Zero Advertising & Zero Third-Party SDKs

The Application is 100% freeware:

  • Zero Ads: We do not display advertisements.
  • Zero Trackers / Analytics: We do not embed analytics SDKs, user behavior trackers, or third-party telemetry tools.
  • Zero Monetization Tracking: Complete functionality is available immediately without accounts, subscriptions, paywalls, or logins.

3. Permissions & Device Access

The Application requests only minimal, standard Android platform permissions strictly required to perform local utility operations:

  • Haptic Vibration (`VIBRATE`): Used strictly to provide tactile haptic confirmation when interacting with controls or stepping through sequences.
  • Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Used solely to alert you when on-device timers or background monitors finish. Notification content is never transmitted across any network.
  • Any requested permissions are executed strictly on-device to power the requested utility features. The Application never sells, brokers, or exfiltrates permission-derived data.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Compliance)

The Application is designed as a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not collect or process personal data from any user, including children.
